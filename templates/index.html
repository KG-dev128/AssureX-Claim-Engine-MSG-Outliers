Enterprise FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the dual-inference machine learning consensus model adjudicate warranty claims? ▾

The platform executes two independent AI pipelines simultaneously: a Tabular Random Forest Classifier trained on over 16 verified warranty risk attributes (product age, damage severity, authorized retailer authority, and historical failure frequency) achieving 94.7% validation accuracy, and a Computer Vision Deep Learning Classifier that evaluates user-submitted damage photography to identify authentic mechanical defects versus excluded external impact fractures. Both inferences are surfaced side-by-side with an automated concordance score for the human adjudicator.

What deterministic rules govern statutory grace periods, damage exclusions, and claim caps? ▾

AssureX enforces a rigid 14-Rule Deterministic Evaluation Matrix (R1–R14). Rules R1–R3 ensure the filing falls strictly within contractual warranty dates, statutory 30-day incident notification windows, and product lifecycle eligibility. Rule R4 verifies that the reported defect matches specified warranty policy clauses. Rules R9–R11 enforce authorized service center maintenance histories and restrict lifetime cumulative claims to the policy cap (maximum 3 prior claims). Failure of any mandatory rule automatically routes the claim to manual adjudicator review.

What optical document evidence is required to satisfy Rule R5 and R8 serial reconciliation? ▾

Under Rule R5 (Proof of Purchase), the claimant must upload an authentic retailer invoice or purchase receipt. The system utilizes Google Gemini OCR to extract transaction dates, authorized vendor names, and monetary values. Under Rule R8, an image of the physical chassis serial plate is parsed and reconciled against the database master record to prevent serial substitution and duplicate claims across multiple service hubs.

How are dispute adjudications, authorized service dispatches, and OEM replacement units coordinated? ▾

Once approved, the claim record is forwarded to the Service Center Portal. Certified technicians log diagnostic seals, component replacements, and repair outcomes. If an item is determined to be non-repairable, the system initiates an OEM replacement unit dispatch workflow under Rule R11, tracking the surrender and return of the defective hardware.

What data privacy and encryption frameworks safeguard customer invoices and telemetry? ▾

All data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 GCM, and all transport traffic requires TLS 1.3. Uploaded diagnostic images and invoices are securely stored in segregated directories with strict authorization tokens. System actions, role elevations, and claim decisions are permanently logged to an immutable PostgreSQL audit trail in compliance with SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 requirements.

How do enterprise administrators assign role-based access privileges and audit system actions? ▾

AssureX implements strict separation of duties. All public self-registrations default to the Customer role. Only authenticated System Administrators can assign elevated roles (Claims Reviewer, Service Technician, Administrator) via the User Role Management panel. Each user's interface dynamically displays only their authorized workspace and prevents cross-department data exposure.

Standardized Operational Procedure

How It Works: End-to-End Adjudication Architecture

From user intake through automated dual-model evaluation and certified adjudicator review, AssureX provides total transparency across the complete warranty lifecycle.

01
📝

Digital Intake & Document OCR Ingestion

Claimant registers product details (serial number, purchase date, authorized retailer) and provides optical proof of purchase alongside fault photographs. The Google Gemini OCR engine parses invoice metadata, validates vendor credibility, and extracts serial numbers in under 800 milliseconds.

02
🔍

Dual-Model Machine Learning Consensus Scoring

The claim payload triggers parallel AI pipelines: the Tabular Random Forest Model calculates underwriting probability (94.7% accuracy) based on product failure telemetry and retailer history, while the Computer Vision Classifier inspects visual fracture geometry to verify genuine hardware component failure.

03
🛡️

14-Rule Deterministic Evaluation Matrix

The system evaluates 14 statutory and policy rules (R1–R14), checking warranty coverage windows, 30-day reporting windows, product age validity, repair frequency caps, and cross-center claim deduplication. Any rule anomaly triggers flagged review.

04
⚖️

Human-in-the-Loop Adjudicator Determination

Certified adjudicators review the AI concordance, inspect optical evidence, and render a legally binding decision (Approve, Reject, or Request Further Evidence). Adjudicators can generate cryptographically signed PDF audit certificates.

05
✨

Authorized Service Dispatch & Fulfillment

Approved claims immediately dispatch work orders to certified OEM service centers or trigger advanced replacement unit logistics. Claimants track repair stages, technician notes, and delivery tracking in real-time.

Statutory Consumer Warranty Protections

AssureX strictly adheres to regional consumer warranty statutes, including the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act in North America and EU Directive 2019/771. Claimants are entitled to free repair or replacement for goods failing to conform to contract specifications within statutory periods, independent of voluntary manufacturer warranties.

Cryptographic Architecture & Access Governance

PostgreSQL Immutable Auditing

Every user authentication, privilege elevation, adjudicator decision, and OCR parse operation is written to an append-only audit trail in PostgreSQL (NeonDB). Logs record user ID, event timestamp, entity ID, action type, and client IP address.

JWT Token Revocation & Blocklist

All sessions operate on short-lived JSON Web Tokens (JWT) with cryptographic signatures. Token IDs (jti) are validated against an in-memory and database blocklist upon every request to ensure immediate revocation on sign-out.

Document Isolation

Uploaded invoices and diagnostic photographs are stored in isolated, cryptographically partitioned directories. Static access requires validated authorization claims; unauthenticated direct access is blocked by the gateway.
